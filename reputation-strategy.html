Reputation Strategy

What is reputation strategy?

The work of deciding what you should become known for — and building the clarity that makes it unmistakable.

Reputation strategy is the discipline of defining what you should be known for, then shaping the narrative, messaging and platform so the right people can understand, believe and remember it.

It is not the same as personal branding. Personal branding and visibility-first approaches often begin with amplification — posting more, showing up more, optimizing for attention. Reputation strategy begins with clarity: who you are, what only you can own, and what that should make possible.

Exceptional work does not automatically create an exceptional reputation. Too often there is a gap between your potential, what you have accomplished and how you are understood. Reputation strategy closes that gap.

Traditional personal branding often starts with visibility. GameChanger starts with clarity — substance before visibility, strategy before amplification, reputation before attention.
